# Starter images and templates

We have prepared artwork to help you make a first story without searching for images. **The Small Adventure pack contains 20 AI-generated images.** Demo 0.8.0 includes the image pack and starter catalog. Choose the content you need and add it to your project.

| Character portraits | 3 | Pathfinder, Archivist, Market Trader |
| Enemies / creatures | 2 | Grey Wolf, Stone Guardian |

| Abilities | 2 | Mending Light, Small Flame |
| Conditions | 2 | Poisoned, Stunned |

The editor includes the complete pack for offline use. Selected images are copied into your project; exported games include the images they actually use. These twenty images do not include audio, animation, directional sprites, tilesets, or a complete screen theme.

Artwork and rules are separate. Choosing a wolf does not start combat or create hostility. A vial, lantern, or ability image does not define healing, vision, or MP costs. Portraits and full-body images should use suitable slots, while condition names, durations, and stacks remain readable data beside the art.

A map image is also distinct from movement, vision, and tactical rules. Current creation maps and pins are separate from the planned interactive player map.

## One place to choose a starting point

The launcher offers **New project / Starter Templates / Open project**, recent projects, and Git cloning. **Starter Templates** lets you search and filter the supplied story, rules, and combat samples and components, then review content, images, dependencies, and notices.

**New project → Blank project / Start from a template** lets you choose how to begin. The latter opens the same catalog rather than a separate quick-start or sample list. Full-project templates create a new project; additive components identify the current project as their target. [The three recommended configurations](CREATION_JOURNEY.md) are planned in this same catalog.

## Remove and restore built-in templates

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Delete from the catalog | Remove this creator's local registration, installed copy, and ordinary listing; preserve existing projects and teammates' lists |
| Delete or replace project content | Review selected copies and references; restore through editing undo |
| Restore deleted starter templates | Reinstall selected bundled originals without replacing projects or modified copies |

Removal persists through restarts and updates, without automatic reinstallation. Empty-project creation and opening existing projects remain available even after every template is removed. Bundled originals may remain as offline recovery resources. Catalog updates do not overwrite images or content already copied into your project.

## First story: A Small Errand

The story meets the Pathfinder and Archivist at a camp, investigates, and makes choices **without mandatory dice or combat**. It selectively uses the camp, two portraits, vial, and key; the remaining fifteen images can be added separately. Excluding images preserves the same text-based story.

Open **Starter Templates → A Small Errand**. Conversation and investigation lead to two possible endings.

## Finalized starter-image usage terms

The 20 originals in A Small Adventure 1.1.0 use **Starter Image Pack Usage Terms 1.0**. Creators may copy, modify, or replace images in projects; share copies needed for team production or project transfer; and include original or modified images in free or paid games and promotional materials for those games.

The attribution file bundled with a game must preserve pack ID `narrafield.starter-fantasy`, the version, generation source `image_gen`, and these terms. Modified copies retain the original source attribution and remain distinguishable from the originals. A separate in-game attribution banner is not required.

The terms do not grant permission to resell the original pack by itself as an image product, guarantee third-party rights involving external brands or people, or certify exclusive rights. Image permissions are separate from engine, SDK, and external rule-pack terms. The demo includes the original pack and usage terms. The official product store is not open yet.
