# Starter images and one template catalog

We have prepared artwork to help you make a first story without searching for images. **The Small Adventure pack contains 20 AI-generated images.** The originals and usage terms are ready, but installer bundling, editor integration, and templates are still in development. The current demo does not include this pack or the new catalog.

## Twenty starter images

| Category | Count | Prepared artwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maps | 2 | Windpath Coast, Ruined Courtyard |
| Scene backgrounds | 3 | Forest Trail Camp, Wayside Inn, Underground Hall |
| Character portraits | 3 | Pathfinder, Archivist, Market Trader |
| Enemies / creatures | 2 | Grey Wolf, Stone Guardian |
| Items | 6 | Traveler's Sword, Amber Vial, Old Key, Wooden Round Shield, Travel Lantern, Coin Pouch |
| Abilities | 2 | Mending Light, Small Flame |
| Conditions | 2 | Poisoned, Stunned |

The plan bundles the complete pack with the editor for offline use, copies selected images into a project, and includes required material in an exported game. These twenty images do not include audio, animation, directional sprites, tilesets, or a complete screen theme.

Artwork and rules are separate. Choosing a wolf does not start combat or create hostility. A vial, lantern, or ability image does not define healing, vision, or MP costs. Portraits and full-body images should use suitable slots, while condition names, durations, and stacks remain readable data beside the art.

A map image is also distinct from movement, vision, and tactical rules. Current creation maps and pins are separate from the planned interactive player map.

## One place to choose a starting point

The planned home screen offers **New project / Starter Templates / Open project**, recent projects, and Git cloning. **Starter Templates** contains story, rules, combat, and screen filters with previews of content, images, dependencies, and notices.

**New project → Empty project / Start from a template** lets you choose how to begin. The latter opens the same catalog rather than a separate quick-start or sample list. Full-project templates create a new project; additive components identify the current project as their target. [The three recommended configurations](CREATION_JOURNEY.md) are planned in this same catalog.

## Remove and restore built-in templates

| Action | Planned effect |
| --- | --- |
| Delete from the catalog | Remove this creator's local registration, installed copy, and ordinary listing; preserve existing projects and teammates' lists |
| Delete or replace project content | Review selected copies and references; restore through editing undo |
| Restore deleted starter templates | Reinstall selected bundled originals without replacing projects or modified copies |

Removal should persist through restarts and updates, without automatic reinstallation. Empty-project creation and opening existing projects remain available even after every template is removed. Bundled originals may remain as offline recovery resources. A new pack version should not overwrite modified artwork or project content.

## First story: A Small Errand

The planned story meets the Pathfinder and Archivist at a camp, investigates, and makes choices **without mandatory dice or combat**. It selectively uses the camp, two portraits, vial, and key; the remaining fifteen images can be added separately. Excluding images should preserve the same text-based story.

This template is still being made. The images are ready; the playable sample will follow.

## Finalized starter-image usage terms

The 20 originals in A Small Adventure 1.1.0 use **Starter Image Pack Usage Terms 1.0**. Creators may copy, modify, or replace images in projects; share copies needed for team production or project transfer; and include original or modified images in free or paid games and promotional materials for those games.

The attribution file bundled with a game must preserve pack ID `narrafield.starter-fantasy`, the version, generation source `image_gen`, and these terms. Modified copies retain the original source attribution and remain distinguishable from the originals. A separate in-game attribution banner is not required.

The terms do not grant permission to resell the original pack by itself as an image product, guarantee third-party rights involving external brands or people, or certify exclusive rights. Image permissions are separate from engine, SDK, and external rule-pack terms. The usage terms are finalized, but installer and store distribution are not ready yet.
